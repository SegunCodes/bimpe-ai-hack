## Intent

The user asked for "a dynamic 30-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion designer you are", focused on the product built in this codebase, with the video content written by us. It plays muted on loop in the hero of the Bimpe marketing page, so every beat must read without sound and the last frame must sit well against the first.

Tellero (renamed from Bimpe on 2026-10-03; the agent itself is also called Tellero) is an AI phone agent: business owners add an order and pick when Bimpe should call (e.g. 2 hours before delivery). Bimpe phones the customer, confirms they are home, turns a vague address ("by the yellow gate") into a clean one with a landmark, reschedules when needed, retries no-answers twice, and onboards new customers in English, Pidgin, Yorùbá, Hausa or Igbo.

- Brand from the site: ink #0b1220, danfo yellow #ffc20e (background/highlight only), indigo #4f46e5 (product actions), green #16a34a for "confirmed". Display font Bricolage Grotesque, body Inter.
